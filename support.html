Support

Help with Pyrograph and other ColdFire Games apps.

Contact us

Email support@coldfiregames.app. Tell us which game you're playing, your device and what happened, and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

How do I play? Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

What is the daily challenge? Once a day, everyone plays the same set of pieces. Your best score counts toward the daily leaderboard and your weekly league.

Can I change my leaderboard name? Yes. Open the daily challenge and tap "Change name".

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads. Removing ads stops ads that appear on their own. Ads you choose to watch for a reward (like an extra attempt) stay available. Reinstalled? Use "Restore purchases".

Delete your data

To delete the data linked to your game (your scores, leaderboard name and league history), email support@coldfiregames.app with the subject "Delete my data". Include your leaderboard name if you set one. We'll confirm when it's done. See our privacy policy for details.
