Pyrograph: burn your way to a clear board

A calm, clever wood block puzzle. Fit the pieces, complete rows and columns, and watch every line you clear catch fire and leave its mark. Each game burns its own picture into the wood.

In development Free to play

Simple to learn, hard to put down

No timers and no rush. Just you, an 8×8 board and three pieces at a time.

🔥

Lines that burn

Clear a row or column and it goes up in embers. Chain clears together to stoke a bigger fire.

✦

The ember tile

Score well to earn a glowing ember that becomes whatever piece fits best, right when you need it.

🏆

Daily challenge & leagues

Everyone gets the same pieces each day. Climb from Bronze to Diamond in weekly leagues.
